## Protocol

- LEO orbit, two-body + J2 + exponential drag
- Sparse tracking: up to 3 passes over 24 h above 10 deg elevation, 8 range (10 m) and Doppler (1 mm/s) samples per pass
- Truth: a = 6900 +/- 10 km, e = 0.002, i uniform in 30-60 deg, node/argp/anomaly uniform
- Initial guess perturbed by 0.5 km and 0.5 m/s per axis
- Bayesian state is whitened by the BLS covariance: u = u_BLS + 10.0 * L * z, so the prior is 10.0x wider than the BLS covariance
- 50 replications, common random seed 42
- BLS: Levenberg-Marquardt, Gauss-Newton covariance, trials dropped when the fit does not converge or the normalized post-fit residual RMS exceeds 2
- Bayes: NUTS, 500 draws after 500 adaptation, target acceptance 0.90
- Software: Julia 1.12.6, Turing 0.49.0, BayesianOrbitDetermination 0.1.0
